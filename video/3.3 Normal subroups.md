# Normal subgroups

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xptnXnbqpPY

A subgroup H of G is a normal subgroup of G if gH = Hg for all g \in G.

Subgroups of abelian groups are always normal.

If H is a subgroup of G of index [G : H] = 2, then H is a normal subgroup of G.

## Conjugate subgroups

For a fixed element g \in G, the set gHg^(-1) = {ghg^(-1) | h \in H} is the conjugate of H by g. 

The conjugate is a subgroup.

## How to check if a subgroup is normal

If gH = Hg then right-multiplying both sides by g^(-1) yields gHg^(-1) = H.

1. `gH = Hg for all g \in G`
2. `gHg^(-1) = H for all \g in G`
3. `ghg^(-1) \in H for all g \in G`
